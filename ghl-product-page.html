The Story Behind the Brand

Born From The Halls of Delaware State.

In 2015, two HBCU graduates — DeShawn Robbins Jr. and Akeem Pridgen — turned a shared vision into a movement. What started as a dream in a dorm room became You Live and You Learn Clothing.

YL² isn't just clothing. It's a philosophy: every setback is a setup, every lesson makes you stronger, and loyalty runs deeper than blood.

This collection is the next chapter — representing the voice of a generation. Raw, unapologetic, and real.
